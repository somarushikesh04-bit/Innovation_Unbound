report Known Scam Patterns in India (RBI/MeitY Report 2024)

🏦
KYC Update Scam
Fake bank agents claim your account will be blocked unless you update KYC via a link or phone call. Banks NEVER ask for OTP or passwords.
Top scam 2024
📞
OTP Phishing
Fraudsters pose as bank support and ask you to share OTP. Real banks will NEVER call and ask for OTP. Sharing OTP = losing money instantly.
Most common
🎰
Prize/Lottery Fraud
You're told you've won a lottery or prize. To claim it, pay a "processing fee." There are no winnings. The fee goes to the scammer.
₹48,200 avg loss
⚡
Electricity Bill Scam
WhatsApp/SMS says your electricity will be cut in 2 hours. Pay via UPI to avoid disconnection. Electricity boards never contact via WhatsApp.
2nd fastest growing
🏛️
TRAI/CBI Impersonation
Caller claims to be from TRAI, CBI, or Narcotics. They threaten legal action unless you pay immediately. Government agencies never demand money over phone.
Highest amount lost
💼
Job/WFH Scam
Work-from-home job offers requiring a "registration fee" or "training fee" upfront. Legitimate employers never ask you to pay to get a job.
Targets youth
